# Changelog

## v0.1.0 — 2026-10-06

First release.

- `custom:phone-pager-card`: one-screen pager for a Home Assistant dashboard. Pages side by side with snap scrolling, a frosted nav pill, pull-to-refresh, a time-of-day wallpaper with per-user presets, a hidden Settings sheet, fade-and-glide for cards and sections that come and go, and frosted more-info dialogs while the dashboard is open.
- `custom:phone-now-playing-card`: lock-screen style now-playing card for one media player.
- `custom:phone-wallpaper-card`: the wallpaper picker used on the Settings sheet.
- README: the full build guide (prompts for Claude, style recipes, pitfalls).
